WHO I AM

Sachin Tekriwal

Sachin Tekriwal is an AI Engineer, AI Scientist and Teacher based in Mumbai, India, with 20+ years of experience in enterprise software and applied AI. He holds an MCA from MGU and an Advanced Diploma from IIIT Bangalore, and currently serves as CTO at NKB Workers.

He approaches every challenge as an architecture problem — understanding its structure, identifying inefficiencies, and engineering solutions that compound over time. His work sits at the intersection of intelligence and automation, guided by a simple philosophy: reduce friction, increase signal, automate deliberately.

Alongside his engineering work, he teaches — helping learners move from curiosity to genuine, applied AI capability.
